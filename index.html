Flagship Game – The South Side

A story-driven action adventure set in the south of India, with a strong focus on life and vehicle simulation.

Game Overview

The South Side explores the tension between discipline and everyday civilian life. Players move through dense environments inspired by Kerala, combining narrative choices with grounded, physics-based gameplay.

Specifications

  • Optimised 3D pipeline and assets
  • Rich natural environments
  • Detailed vehicles and props

Audience

Designed for both casual and dedicated players, balancing approachable controls with depth and replay value.
